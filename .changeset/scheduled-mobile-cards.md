Subscriptions now show as cards with separate Income and Outgoing totals for the next 7 days. The account filter applies to the list, totals and calendar, the calendar collapses behind a "Show calendar" button on phones, and a Sort menu replaces the column headers. Tapping a subscription opens its details; recording a payment is always an explicit action, and the editor and Record payment screens open as drawers on phones.
